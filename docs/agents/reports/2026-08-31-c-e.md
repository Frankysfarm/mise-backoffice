# Packet C-E — Lagerplan und QR-Lagerplätze

## AUDIT

### Existed

- `inventory_areas` is the location-scoped room/area model; `inventory_shelves` is the physical shelf/place model already referenced by `inventory_items.shelf_id`.
- `inventory_items.letzte_inventur`, Soll-/Mindestbestand, suppliers and `generate_reorder_list` already drive stock status and supplier-grouped reorder proposals.
- `stock_movements` is the canonical audit ledger. `inventory_sessions`, `inventory_counts` and `complete_inventory_session` already implement assigned blind counts and deviations.
- Inventory dashboard, products, suppliers, movements, sessions, receiving, waste and order drafts were already exposed below `/neo/app/lager`.
- `/api/qr/[slug]` and print infrastructure existed for storefront/table use, but encoded tenant storefront redirects rather than authenticated storage places.

### Hidden / hard to use

- Shelves were a flat management list. Product count, stock status, inspection age and mobile actions were not visible by physical place.
- Existing item-to-shelf assignments were usable during inventory but had no scan destination or printable place label.
- Auto-reorder already grouped drafts by supplier, but the physical place context was absent from the overview.

### Missing

- Room → typed unit → place hierarchy and cycle/scope validation.
- Tenant-scoped, authenticated QR destination per place.
- Mobile book, withdraw, transfer and count actions from a scanned place.
- Atomic transfer ledger pair and last-check actor/time on the place.
- Desktop/mobile contract coverage for the new protected surfaces.

## DB CHANGES

- Migration: `supabase/migrations/20260831140000_inventory_warehouse_plan.sql` (additive, explicit transaction).
- `inventory_shelves` columns: `parent_shelf_id`, `place_kind`, `unit_type`, `qr_token`, `last_checked_at`, `last_checked_by`.
- Constraints/indexes: kind/type checks, unique opaque QR token, parent index, hierarchy validation trigger (same room, unit→place, no self/cycles).
- Existing flat shelves are retained as places and backfilled below one “Bestehende Lagerplätze” unit per room; existing product assignments are preserved.
- Service-role-only RPC `save_inventory_place_as_actor`: validates active manager/backoffice/admin actor plus tenant/location/area before write.
- Service-role-only RPC `record_inventory_place_action_as_actor`: validates active actor and tenant/location/place/item, locks stock, rejects negative stock, records every action. Transfer requires the complete place stock and atomically writes an outgoing and incoming ledger row.
- Existing RLS remains authoritative (`inventory_shelves` → area → location → tenant); no duplicate inventory table or employee identity was introduced.
- Rollback: drop the two RPCs and validation trigger, then drop the six additive columns (which also removes their indexes/FKs). Before dropping, move any products assigned to newly created places to retained legacy places. No production/database operation was run in this packet because the factory has no database.

## OPEN ITEMS

- The factory has no `.env`, database or authenticated fixture. Therefore the migration/RPCs were contract-tested and compiled but not executed against Supabase, and the authenticated create→scan→book/withdraw/count browser flow cannot honestly be claimed as live-tested here. The Playwright spec covers both protected screens and mutation authentication in desktop/mobile projects and is ready to extend with the integration environment's authenticated storage state.
- Reorder creation continues to use the existing item-level Soll/minimum and supplier grouping. Because the current authoritative model assigns one item row to one shelf, a transfer moves the complete recorded place stock; splitting one product across multiple places would require a future normalized placement-balance model and was intentionally not introduced as a parallel stock system.
- Existing repository lint warnings shown during `next build` remain outside this packet; no new packet-file warning was emitted.

## HOW TO VERIFY MANUALLY

### Owner — desktop

1. Sign in as Unternehmer/Admin and open `Neo → Lager → Lagerplan`.
2. Confirm every prior flat shelf appears below “Bestehende Lagerplätze” after migration.
3. In a room, create a Regal/Kühlschrank/Gefrierschrank, then create a named place inside it.
4. Open a place and confirm product count plus `ok`, `nachbestellen`, `Inventur fällig` or `leer` chips.
5. Open “QR-Etikett öffnen und drucken”; scan the printed SVG and confirm it opens the same tenant-scoped place only after login.
6. Use Einbuchen, Entnehmen, Umlagern and Zählen; verify stock, last control actor/time and Lager → Bewegungen. Verify transfer produces matching outgoing/incoming rows.
7. Lower a product below minimum and confirm the existing Lager dashboard/order flow groups the proposal by supplier.

### Staff — phone

1. Sign in with an active employee at the place's location and scan its label.
2. Confirm large touch actions and the German product/Soll/Ist/reorder/last-control information fit without horizontal scrolling.
3. Book stock, withdraw stock, count a deviation and transfer the complete place stock to a searched target.
4. Confirm negative withdrawals, foreign-tenant tokens and places outside the employee location are rejected.
